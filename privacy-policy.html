Privacy Policy

Summary

Hazard Flashing Lights does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your device with no network connection required.

What We Collect

Nothing. The app does not collect any data of any kind.

No personal information
No location data
No analytics
No crash reports
No device identifiers
No usage tracking
No third-party SDKs
No account required

Device Permissions

The app requests permission to adjust screen brightness. This is used solely to maximise brightness when the lights are active, improving visibility. The original brightness is restored when you stop. This value never leaves your device.

Third Parties

No data is shared with or transmitted to any third party. The app makes no network requests.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
